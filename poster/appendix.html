B. Reproducibility

Code, prompts, the 195 CLMET sentence pairs and all raw model outputs: REPO_URL.

  1. run_polbix.py — prompts both models (temperature 0, 3 runs, max 5 output tokens) with the original German prompts (PolBiX) or their English translations (CLMET); rate-limited, resumable.
  2. build_clmet_pairs.py — samples candidate sentences from CLMET 3.1 and swaps one noun; the final 195 pairs were then reviewed one by one (AI-assisted) for word sense and grammaticality (clmet_pairs.csv).
  3. poster/stats.py, poster/make_poster.py — Δ P(true), bootstrap CIs and the authors’ logistic regression; builds this poster.

Models: gpt-4.1-mini (OpenAI API) and claude-haiku-4-5-20251001 (Anthropic API), queried 28 September 2026.
